Boot: initramfs, splash, login screen
Installs ${PKG_BOOT[*]}
initramfs-tools with MODULES=dep, plymouth g0wm, 'quiet splash' in GRUB.
Kernel arguments go in /etc/default/grub.d, /etc/default/grub stays as it is. / on LUKS: checked that cryptsetup-initramfs and /etc/crypttab unlock it, an initramfs without it is not kept.
GRUB theme minimal (tomdewildt, fixed commit): GRUB_THEME in /etc/default/grub.d, the arguments the system booted with (root, subvolume, resume) are checked in grub.cfg.
No 'Loading Linux ...' lines after the menu (10_linux patched; dpkg keeps it and leaves the new one of a grub update as 10_linux.dpkg-dist, maint patches that and puts it in place).
tty1: the cat of /etc/issue and the login in a frame in the middle of the screen (g0wm-login), the clock ticks every minute. The cat wakes up for the password (/etc/g0wm-login/awake) with "Caps Lock is on" if it is, login(1) still asks it. The Caps Lock line follows the key while the password is typed, the clock goes on. One try per login (LOGIN_RETRIES 1 in /etc/login.defs): a wrong password makes the cat grumpy (/etc/g0wm-login/grumpy) with "Login incorrect" under the prompts at once after the error (FAIL_DELAY 0). pam_faillock in common-auth (pam-auth-update, profiles dotfiles-faillock*): 5 wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock the account out for 10 on the ttys, gtklock, doas and su, root never; the cat says until when and asks no password meanwhile. A session where g0wm crashed or exited with an error shows on the next login screen ("g0wm crashed: SEGV · logs session"). A new screen size (font, monitor) draws the frame again in the middle. After the login the cat stays until g0wm draws ("waking up g0wm…", g0wm-login --starting from ~/.bash_profile). The battery sits in the bottom of the frame and ticks with the clock; the first draw of the boot comes a line at a time and turns Num Lock on. The login(1) of Debian writes no btmp and ends with 0 on a wrong password too: g0wm-login reads the records of pam_faillock.
The splash stays until that screen draws over it (plymouth quit --retain-splash), on the same #0a0a0a: no black in between. No motd or last login after the password (~/.hushlogin), a blank screen until g0wm draws.
The 16 colors of every tty from the theme, set by the kernel (vt.default_red/grn/blu): /etc/issue in the colors of foot, brown in the amber of the theme. loglevel=3: driver errors go to the journal, not over the login.
Terminus font on every tty (console-setup), larger on screens 1440 pixels tall or more.
Rebuilds the initramfs when needed, old images restored on failure.
